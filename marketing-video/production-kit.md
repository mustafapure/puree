## Shot list — 5 clips × 5 seconds

### Shot 1 · 0–5s · "The Spark"
> Close-up of the hero boy at a wooden architect's desk covered in blueprints, warm lamp glow, sunset city visible through the window behind him. He looks up from his sketchbook — his pencil drawing of a skyline begins to glow with golden light and lifts off the page as particles. Camera slowly pushes in toward the window. **End frame: camera passing through the window glass toward the glowing city.**

*Transition hook → shot 2: push-through-window.*

### Shot 2 · 5–10s · "The Run Begins"
> The hero boy bursts through a doorway onto a sunset city street and sprints left-to-right, backpack bouncing, huge grin. Anime speed lines and motion blur on the background, camera tracking sideways at his pace past storefronts and lampposts, warm rim light on his hair. **Start frame: door swinging open with light spilling out. End frame: he dashes behind a concrete pillar filling the frame.**

*Transition hook → shot 3: pillar wipe / whip-pan.*

### Shot 3 · 10–15s · "Between the Buildings"
> Low-angle dolly shot rushing through a narrow alley between towering skyscrapers at golden hour, the hero boy leaping over a puddle that reflects the glowing towers and orange sky. Sheets of blueprint paper and petals swirl in his wake, dramatic perspective lines, lens flare between the buildings. **End frame: a blueprint sheet flies directly into the camera lens, filling the frame with white paper.**

*Transition hook → shot 4: paper whiteout.*

### Shot 4 · 15–20s · "The Ascent"
> The hero boy sprints up an open exterior staircase toward a rooftop, the city skyline opening up wider behind him with every step, wind ruffling his hair, determined smile toward the camera. Camera cranes upward with him, warm sun flare growing at the top of the stairs. **End frame: he runs into the sun flare, frame washes to warm white.**

*Transition hook → shot 5: sun-flare whiteout.*

### Shot 5 · 20–25s · "The Hero Shot"
> The hero boy stands at a rooftop edge overlooking a vast sunset skyline, back three-quarter view, wind in his hair, holding his open sketchbook. Glowing golden sketch-lines rise from the page and trace themselves over the real buildings, as if the city is his drawing come to life. Camera slowly pulls back and up, revealing the whole city. Calm, triumphant mood. **Hold the final 1.5s steady for the end-card text overlay.**
