Abstract

Slezin (hypromellose 3 mg + dextran-70 1 mg per millilitre) is a keratoprotective ophthalmic solution indicated for the symptomatic relief of dry-eye syndrome. The viscoelastic polymer system stabilises the precorneal tear film, prolongs tear film breakup time, and protects the corneal epithelium from desiccation. The following document presents the primary packaging, ocular surface anatomy, mechanism of action, clinical evidence over a twelve-week interval, and administration protocol.

Fig. 02 · Ocular surface anatomy

The precorneal tear film.

The tear film is the eye's first refractive surface and its first line of defence against the environment. It is approximately 12 μm thick and is organised into three distinct layers, reconstituted with each blink.

In keratoconjunctivitis sicca, breakup of the film occurs in under five seconds, exposing the corneal epithelium and producing the characteristic burning sensation. Slezin is a viscoelastic polymer system that adheres to the cornea, increases aqueous-layer viscosity, and prolongs tear film breakup time.

Fig. 02 Sagittal section through the human eye and ocular adnexa. Inset (right) details the three layers of the precorneal tear film.
A · LIPID LAYER · ~0.1 μm
Reduces evaporation.

Meibomian-gland secretion forms a superficial oily monolayer that seals the aqueous phase against the air interface.

B · AQUEOUS LAYER · ~7 μm
Maintains hydration.

The principal volume of the film. In aqueous-deficient dry eye it falls below the threshold required to maintain corneal wetting between blinks.

C · MUCIN LAYER · ~0.5 μm
Anchors to the cornea.

A glycoprotein gel produced by conjunctival goblet cells renders the hydrophobic corneal epithelium wettable by the aqueous phase.

Fig. 03 · Active components

Mechanism of action.

Hypromellose is a semi-synthetic cellulose ether; dextran-70 is a branched α-1,6 glucan of approximately 70 kDa. Together they increase tear-phase viscosity, reduce surface tension, and adhere to the corneal surface — prolonging tear film breakup time without sustained visual disturbance.

A · ACTIVE SUBSTANCEHypromellose 3 mg/ml
HPMC · hydroxypropyl methylcellulose · (C₃₂H₆₀O₁₉)ₙ

A semi-synthetic, viscoelastic cellulose ether that forms a colloid solution in water. Stabilises the precorneal tear film, increases viscosity of the aqueous phase and prolongs film breakup time.

B · ACTIVE SUBSTANCEDextran-70 · 1 mg/ml
Branched α-1,6 glucan · (C₆H₁₀O₅)ₙ · ~70 kDa

A branched glucan polysaccharide. Increases tear viscosity and corneal residence time. Acts synergistically with hypromellose to extend the tear film breakup interval.

Fig. 04 · Tear film, before and after instillation

Stabilisation of the precorneal film.

In the dry eye the aqueous layer is insufficient and the film breaks up within seconds, leaving discrete areas of corneal exposure. After instillation of one drop of Slezin, the viscoelastic polymer network thickens the aqueous phase, anchors to the mucin layer, and reconstitutes a stable continuous film.

Tab. 01 · 12-week efficacy outcomes

Clinical evidence.

In a 12-week prospective study (Cömez et al., 2013) of patients with dry-eye syndrome, a hypromellose–dextran formulation produced statistically significant improvements across all four standard tear function endpoints: ocular surface disease index, tear osmolarity, Schirmer test, and tear film breakup time.

Fig. 05 · Administration protocol

Posology and method of administration.

Topical ocular use. Instill one to two drops into the conjunctival sac as required. Slezin is pharmacologically inert; use is permitted in pregnancy and lactation. A 15 ml multidose vial delivers approximately 300 drops. Discard four weeks after first opening.

Step 01 · Preparation
Tilt the head back.

Remove contact lenses prior to administration. Tilt the head backwards and gently retract the lower eyelid with a clean finger to expose the conjunctival sac.

Step 02 · Instillation
Instill one drop.

Hold the bottle above the eye without contact with the lashes or ocular surface. Instill one drop into the conjunctival sac. A second drop may be applied if symptoms persist.

Step 03 · Distribution
Close the eye briefly.

Close the eyelids for a few seconds to allow the polymer solution to distribute uniformly across the corneal surface. Repeat as required; discard four weeks after first opening.

Slezin is considered a first-line agent in the management of dry-eye syndrome owing to its safety profile, dosing flexibility and cost per drop. The 15 ml format yields roughly 300 drops per vial — a practical economic argument in chronic management.
